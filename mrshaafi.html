About Us

Dedicated to Student Success in Tangail

Mr. Shaafi Coaching Center has been a cornerstone of academic excellence in Tangail for over eight years. We believe every student can achieve their full potential with the right guidance.

Our Journey

2016
Founded in Tangail with a single SSC batch of 12 students.
2018
Expanded to HSC courses; first batch of students scored GPA 5.0.
2020
Introduced online classes during pandemic; maintained 90%+ attendance.
2022
Moved to larger premises in Tangail Sadar. Capacity doubled.
2024
Over 500 students taught; 95% SSC/HSC pass rate achieved.
